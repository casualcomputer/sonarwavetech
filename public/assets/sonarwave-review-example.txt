ILLUSTRATIVE GPU PURCHASE REVIEW

This is an example of the review format, not a client report or a hardware recommendation. Your review is based on your workloads, quotes and agreed scope.

Example brief: a team wants a local AI assistant for internal documents and is comparing GPU server quotes.

1. Requirements
Record the product vision, required features, models, developer expertise and existing software. Connect these to user demand, response-time needs, data restrictions and budget.

2. Quote assessment
Assess whether the proposed hardware supports the required models and software, beyond GPU memory alone. Check facilities, networking, storage, warranty and vendor assumptions.

3. Installation readiness
Check power, cooling, rack or workstation space, network access and who owns installation and system access.

4. Tests on target GPUs, if scoped
Test agreed workloads on the GPUs being considered. Report compatibility findings, response times, request capacity and running costs, with the test conditions and any limits on applying the results to the complete system.

5. Recommendation & validation
Explain the recommended direction, unresolved risks and compatibility and performance checks to complete before ordering. Define what the system must demonstrate during acceptance testing.

The outcome is a decision record: how the system supports your product vision, what the evidence shows and what still needs to be checked before you commit.
